    How it works

    Strength is estimated from length and character variety (entropy = length × log2 of the character pool), then reduced for common passwords, repeated characters, and sequences like "abc" or "123". For reuse prevention, saved passwords are stored only as SHA-256 hashes in your browser's localStorage, never as plain text. Suggestions use crypto.getRandomValues, a cryptographically secure random source.
